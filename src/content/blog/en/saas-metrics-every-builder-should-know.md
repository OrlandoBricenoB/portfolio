"Users are growing" is not a metric. These are the numbers investors, buyers and your own team use to tell whether a software business is healthy. Learn them early; they are much harder to fix late.

## Recurring revenue: MRR and ARR

**MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)** is the predictable revenue you earn each month. It excludes one-off setup fees and consulting. Track its movements:

- **New MRR:** from new customers.
- **Expansion MRR:** upsells and cross-sells.
- **Contraction MRR:** downgrades.
- **Churned MRR:** cancellations.
- **Net New MRR:** New + Expansion − Contraction − Churned.

**ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)** is MRR × 12 (plus annual contracts). It is the number used to value SaaS companies.

## Churn and NRR: are customers staying?

**Churn** is the share you lose in a period. **Logo churn** counts customers; **revenue churn** counts money. Losing 5% of customers monthly means you replace your whole base in under two years.

**NRR (Net Revenue Retention)** is the recurring revenue kept from an existing cohort after expansions and cancellations:

```
NRR = (Starting MRR + Expansion − Contraction − Churned) / Starting MRR
```

Above **100%** you grow even if you sign nobody new. Great B2B SaaS lives at 110–130%.

## Unit economics: CAC, LTV, payback

- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** total sales and marketing spend ÷ new customers in the period.
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** gross profit a customer generates over their whole life. Quick formula: `ARPA × gross margin ÷ monthly churn`.
- **LTV:CAC:** healthy is **3:1 or better**. Below 1:1 you pay to lose money.
- **CAC payback:** months of gross margin needed to recover CAC. Under 12 months is solid for SMB SaaS.

**Example:** CAC $300, ARPA $50/month, 80% margin, 3% monthly churn. LTV = 50 × 0.8 ÷ 0.03 ≈ $1,333. LTV:CAC ≈ 4.4:1. Payback = 300 ÷ 40 = 7.5 months. Healthy.

## ARPU / ARPA and GMV

**ARPU / ARPA** is average monthly revenue per user or account. It tells you whether you should move upmarket or raise prices.

**GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)** is the total volume of transactions flowing through a marketplace or fintech. It is **not your revenue**: your revenue is GMV × **take rate**. A platform with $1M GMV and a 10% take rate earns $100k.

## How you grow: PLG vs. SLG

- **PLG (Product-Led Growth):** the product itself acquires and converts users (free tier, trial, virality). Low price, high volume.
- **SLG (Sales-Led Growth):** a sales team (SDRs, account executives) closes mid-market and enterprise accounts. Higher price, longer cycles.

Many companies start PLG and add sales once accounts get bigger.

## Which ones to track first

If you have one hour a month, track: **MRR growth, logo churn, NRR, CAC payback**. Add the rest as you scale. Always measure by **cohort**; averages hide problems.
