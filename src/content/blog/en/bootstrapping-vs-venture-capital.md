How you fund a company shapes everything: your speed, your control, your lifestyle and your odds. There is no best option, only the one that fits the business you want.

## Bootstrapping and the indie path

- **Bootstrapping:** growing with your own savings and customer cash flow. You keep **100% control**, but growth is limited by what you can afford.
- **Indie hacker:** a founder (alone or in a tiny team) building profitable, high-margin products, choosing freedom and lifestyle over hypergrowth.
- **Micro-SaaS:** a very niche SaaS run by 1–3 people with minimal costs and strong profit. $5k–$30k MRR can be a great life.

Best when the market is modest, the product is cheap to build and you want to own the outcome.

## Startup studios

A **startup studio / venture builder** creates companies systematically, contributing capital, infrastructure and a team in exchange for a significant equity stake (often 20–40%). You get speed and resources; you give up a large share early. Read how decisions are made before you sign.

## Angels and venture capital

- **Angel investor:** an individual (often a former founder) investing their own money at Pre-Seed or Seed. Faster, smaller checks, useful advice.
- **Venture capital (VC):** funds investing other people's money (LPs) looking for **10x–100x** returns. VCs need companies that can become huge, so the model fits ambitious markets and punishes slow, steady ones.

Taking VC money is a **commitment to grow fast and exit**. Make sure that is the life you want.

## Funding rounds

- **Pre-Seed / Seed:** validate the idea, build the MVP, find first traction.
- **Series A:** scale a model with proven product-market fit.
- **Series B, C+:** aggressive expansion, new products, new countries.

Each round sells equity (dilution), so raise only what moves you to the next milestone.

## Non-dilutive funding

Money that does not cost equity:

- **Revenue-based financing (RBF):** repaid as a percentage of revenue.
- **Grants and subsidies.**
- **Cloud and AI credits** from AWS, Google, Microsoft and others.

Underrated for early builders: free credits can extend your runway by months.

## How to choose

| If you want... | Consider |
|---|---|
| Full control and a sustainable income | Bootstrapping, micro-SaaS |
| Help getting started fast | Studio, angels |
| To attack a very large market | VC |
| To stretch cash without selling equity | Non-dilutive funding |
